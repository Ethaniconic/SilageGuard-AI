## 1. Scientific Integrity Principle

> **"Never hide synthetic data. Verify what is actually true, correct what is overstated, and clearly separate software benchmarks from real-world field validation."**

SILAGEGUARD AI V2.1 strictly separates data into four distinct categories:
1. **Synthetic Benchmarks**: Parametrically manufactured data used to benchmark algorithms and verify mobile parsers.
2. **Reference Literature**: Peer-reviewed scientific publications establishing biological bounds and threshold rules.
3. **Field Observations**: Physical on-farm measurements with verified ground-truth sources.
4. **Laboratory Results**: Accredited wet-chemistry gold-standard tests (HPLC, Kjeldahl).

## 2. Dataset Taxonomy & Provenance

### A. Research-Informed Synthetic Sensor Benchmark (`datasets/processed/silage_sensor_v2.csv`)
* **Category**: `SYNTHETIC BENCHMARK`
* **Real or Synthetic**: **100% Synthetic**
* **Sample Count**: 2,400 rows across 60 simulated bunker pits and 10 simulated farms.
* **Generation Method**: Generated by `datasets/generate_realistic_silage_data.py` using Gaussian and uniform distributions informed by agronomic ranges in Kung et al. (2018) and Borreani et al. (2018).
* **Data Circularity Disclosure**:
  * Ground truth labels were computed by evaluating a multi-factor score on `ph`, `moisture`, and `delta_t`.
  * Machine learning models trained on this dataset are learning to approximate that synthetic rule set.
  * **ML accuracy on this dataset (94.38%) reflects benchmark consistency, NOT real-world field accuracy.**
* **Limitations**:
  * Temperature rise is generated algorithmically and does not represent physical core probe measurements.
  * Must never be presented as real-world field evidence.

---

### B. Synthetic Silage Surface Textures (`datasets/vision/`)
* **Category**: `SYNTHETIC PROTOTYPE`
* **Real or Synthetic**: **100% Synthetic**
* **Sample Count**: 160 images (60 Safe, 50 Caution, 50 Unsafe).
* **Generation Method**: Generated procedurally in Python using Pillow (`PIL.ImageDraw`).
  * *Safe*: Drawn green lines simulating chopped forage fibers.
  * *Caution*: Splotches simulating aerobic heating/browning.
  * *Unsafe*: Stippled dots simulating mold hyphae colonies.
* **Evaluation Disclosure**:
  * MobileNetV3 achieves 100% validation accuracy because the procedural generator creates easily separable geometric shapes.
  * **This 100% metric is a synthetic prototype validation score, NOT field computer vision accuracy.**
  * Real outdoor agricultural imagery (sunlight glare, shadow, dust, mud) requires field validation.

### C. Reference Scientific Literature (`datasets/metadata/literature_provenance.json`)
* **Kung et al. (2018)**: *Silage review: Interpretation of chemical, microbial, and organoleptic components of silages*, Journal of Dairy Science, 101(5), 4020–4033. (Establishes pH, dry matter, and VFA targets).
* **Borreani et al. (2018)**: *Silage review: Factors affecting dry matter and quality losses in silages*, Journal of Dairy Science, 101(5), 3952–3979. (Establishes aerobic stability and thermal rise dynamics).
* **Clarification**: These are peer-reviewed review papers, not raw probe telemetry datasets.

---

### D. Real Field Pilot Observations (`datasets/field/field_pilot_observations.csv`)
* **Category**: `REAL PHYSICAL PILOT DATASET`
* **Real or Synthetic**: **Real on-farm observations and standardized schema**
* **Location**: Vidarbha Dairy Belt (Nagpur, Amravati, Wardha districts, Maharashtra).
* **Schema (16 Fields)**:
  `sample_id, farm_id, pit_id, crop_type, silage_age_days, sampling_depth_cm, ph, moisture, temperature, ambient_temperature, image_path, expert_label, lab_result, label_source, timestamp, notes`
* **Missing Value Policy**:
  * Unmeasured values are recorded strictly as `null` or empty.
  * **Never** filled with fabricated or synthetic values.
* **Small Dataset Strategy**:
  * The evaluation architecture supports small cohorts (20+, 50+, 100+ samples) as real observations become available.

## 3. Ground Truth Labeling Hierarchy

Every field sample must specify one of the following six label sources:

| Label Source | Description | Credibility |
|---|---|---|
| `LAB` | Verified by accredited analytical laboratory (HPLC, Kjeldahl, ELISA). | Gold Standard |
| `EXPERT` | Evaluated on-site by a trained dairy veterinarian or nutritionist. | High |
| `RESEARCH` | Peer-reviewed experimental trial ground truth. | High |
| `RULE` | Algorithmic threshold rule based on agronomic bounds. | Rule-based proxy |
| `SYNTHETIC` | Parametrically manufactured for software pipeline testing. | Prototype only |
| `UNKNOWN` | Source cannot be verified (excluded from model training). | Unusable |

---

## 4. Absolute Scientific Boundaries

1. **No Mycotoxin / Aflatoxin Claims**: RGB cameras detect visible surface anomalies, mycelial patterns, and discoloration. They cannot measure chemical mycotoxin or aflatoxin concentrations in ppb.
2. **No Urea Adulteration Claims**: Standard pH electrodes measure hydronium ion activity, not urea concentration.
3. **No Guaranteed Safety**: Results are reported as **"LOW SCREENING RISK (Based on available screening evidence)"**, never as guaranteed safe to feed.
4. **Rapid Screening System**: SILAGEGUARD AI is an on-farm rapid triage tool, not a laboratory replacement.
